FINAL EVALUATION PROTOCOL
=========================

Configurations
--------------
1. Cartographer
2. Cartographer + YOLOv8n
3. RTAB-Map
4. RTAB-Map + YOLOv8n

Repeated trials
---------------
10 analyzed runs per configuration.
40 analyzed runs in total.

Environment
-----------
ROS 2 Humble Hawksbill
Gazebo Classic 11.10.2
TurtleBot3 Waffle Pi
small_house.world

Trajectory representation
-------------------------
Ground-truth and SLAM trajectories were recorded in TUM format.

Before EVO evaluation, trajectories were converted to planar
x-y-yaw representation. z, roll, and pitch were removed.

Timestamp association
---------------------
Maximum permitted timestamp difference:
0.02 s

Unmatched poses were excluded.
No trajectory interpolation was performed.

APE Translation
---------------
evo_ape tum GT EST
--t_max_diff 0.02
--align_origin
-r trans_part

APE Yaw
-------
evo_ape tum GT EST
--t_max_diff 0.02
--align_origin
-r angle_deg

RPE Translation
---------------
evo_rpe tum GT EST
--t_max_diff 0.02
--delta 1
--delta_unit m
--all_pairs
-r trans_part

RPE Yaw
-------
evo_rpe tum GT EST
--t_max_diff 0.02
--delta 1
--delta_unit m
--all_pairs
-r angle_deg

APE uses origin alignment only.
No full SE(3)/Umeyama best-fit alignment is used.

RPE uses no global alignment.

Statistics
----------
Manuscript summary values are reported as mean ± between-run
sample standard deviation across the 10 runs of each configuration.

Ground-truth isolation
----------------------
The Gazebo differential-drive plugin uses:

odometry_source = 0

The /odom topic therefore represents simulated wheel/encoder
odometry.

Independent Gazebo reference poses are published by the separate
gazebo_ros_p3d plugin under /ground_truth.

Neither Cartographer nor RTAB-Map subscribes to
/ground_truth/odom.

YOLO
----
YOLOv8n was executed on CPU.

GPU/CUDA inference was not used in the analyzed trials.

Detection outputs were not supplied to Cartographer or RTAB-Map.

Interpretation
--------------
Trials were manually teleoperated.

The trajectories were not identical replayed trajectories.

Consequently, differences between baseline and YOLO-active
groups are descriptive observations and are not interpreted as
causal estimates of the effect of YOLO workload.
